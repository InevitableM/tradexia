Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) Annual Report FY2024-25 — Excerpt

Revenue Sources

TCS generates revenue primarily through information technology services,
consulting, and business solutions offered to clients across multiple
industry verticals. The company's revenue streams can be broadly categorized
into the following segments:

1. Banking, Financial Services and Insurance (BFSI): This remains the largest
contributor to TCS's revenue, driven by digital transformation initiatives,
core banking modernization, and regulatory compliance projects undertaken by
global financial institutions.

2. Retail and Consumer Business: Revenue from this segment comes from
supply chain optimization, e-commerce platform development, and customer
experience management solutions for retail clients worldwide.

3. Communications, Media and Technology: TCS provides network modernization,
OTT platform engineering, and IT infrastructure management services to
telecom and media companies.

4. Manufacturing: Industrial automation, IoT-enabled smart factory solutions,
and enterprise resource planning implementations form the core of this
segment's revenue.

5. Life Sciences and Healthcare: Revenue here is driven by clinical trial
management systems, healthcare data platforms, and regulatory compliance
software for pharmaceutical and healthcare organizations.

Geographically, North America continues to be the largest market, contributing
a majority of total revenue, followed by Europe, and then the UK and India
markets. The company continues to diversify its revenue base by expanding
presence in emerging markets across Asia Pacific and the Middle East.

Risk Factors

The company's revenue is subject to currency fluctuation risk given its
significant exposure to US Dollar and Euro denominated contracts. Additionally,
client concentration in the BFSI sector means that regulatory changes in
banking and financial services globally could materially impact revenue growth.
